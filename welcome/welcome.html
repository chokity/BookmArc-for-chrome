BookmArc

Favorites, your bookmarks bar as pinned tabs, your open tabs and a command bar, in Chrome’s side panel.

1 Open the sidebar

Pin the extension (puzzle icon in the toolbar), then click its icon or press ⌥⇧A. Chrome keeps the side panel open from then on. To have it on the left: Chrome’s Settings → Appearance → Side panel position.

2 Your bookmarks bar, pinned

The sidebar’s pinned tabs are your bookmarks bar, folders included. Pin, rename, sort or unpin here and the bookmarks change; edit the bookmarks in Chrome and the sidebar follows. With Chrome Sync they reach your other devices too.

3 Find your way

  • Pin a tab by dragging it above the divider, make it a favorite by dragging it to the top. Pinned tabs keep their place when closed.
  • Folders: right-click “Bookmarks” → New Folder, then drag pinned tabs onto it. Double-click a name to rename it.
  • Select several rows with ⇧-click or ⌘-click, then right-click them.
  • Command bar: ⌥⇧T or the “+” above your tabs. ⇥ on a site searches inside it. ⌥⇧L opens it on the current tab’s address, to edit it.
  • Colors and sizes: right-click “Bookmarks” → Edit Theme Color, Sizing.

Chrome doesn’t let extensions hide its tab strip or take over ⌘T / ⌘W.
